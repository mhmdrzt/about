Motor drives & control

XIMA website ↗
  • Developed encoder-based field-oriented control (FOC) for PMSM and induction motors, including current and speed control loops running in real time.
  • Built motor parameter identification and autotuning routines to support commissioning and control-model configuration.
  • Implemented sensorless vector control for general-purpose induction-motor loads such as fans and pumps, with no encoder feedback.
  • Developed an enhanced sensorless control solution for elevators, focused on low-speed behavior, smooth starts and stops, and ride quality.
  • Designed a multi-drive pump network with a master/slave architecture and PID-based pressure control across several VFDs.
  • Built an off-grid solar pump inverter with on-chip MPPT, running directly from photovoltaic input.
  • Integrated Sin/Cos, EnDat, and 5 V/12 V incremental encoders for motor feedback, and implemented IAP-based safe bootloaders for field firmware updates.
